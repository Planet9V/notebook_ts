# Social Media Builder

The Social Media Builder is the content creation hub in Tetrel Notebook. Access it via the **Media** tab in the main navigation.

## What It Does

Turn your research notes and notebooks into ready-to-publish social content:

- **LinkedIn posts** — Professional insights from your research
- **Twitter/X threads** — Multi-part breakdowns of complex topics  
- **Instagram carousels** — Visual slide-by-slide content summaries
- **Newsletter drafts** — Long-form email content from notebook themes

## Getting Started

1. Open the **Media** tab from the left navigation
2. Select the **Social Creator** sub-tab
3. Choose your content source: a notebook, a note, or a free-text prompt
4. Pick your target platform and content format
5. Click **Generate** — the AI drafts content using your research

## Content Sources

| Source Type | Best For |
|---|---|
| **Notebook** | Broad topic summaries, weekly roundups |
| **Note** | Single insight, opinion piece, how-to |
| **Source** | Summary of a document or article |
| **Free text** | Custom prompts, announcements |

## Platform Formats

| Platform | Available Formats |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Professional post, article intro, poll |
| Twitter/X | Single tweet, thread (up to 20 tweets) |
| Instagram | Caption, carousel slides |
| Newsletter | Intro section, full draft |

## Editing & Refinement

After generating:
- Click any section to edit inline
- Use the **Tone** selector to adjust: Professional / Casual / Informative / Provocative
- Click **Regenerate Section** to redo a single part
- Use **Copy** to copy the final content to your clipboard

## Publishing

Tetrel does not post directly to social platforms — it generates draft content for you to review and publish manually. This keeps you in control of your brand voice.

Future versions may include direct publish integrations via the Publication settings.

## Tips

- **More context = better output**: Select a notebook with multiple sources for richer content
- **Use the Tone selector**: "Provocative" often generates more engaging hooks for Twitter/X
- **Iterate quickly**: Regenerating is free — try 2-3 variations before choosing
- **Save your favorites**: Copy generated content to a new Note in your notebook for future reference
